  stats/screenshot and final stats/screenshot after 120–180 host seconds.
  No explicit serial diagnostic command during the timed observation.
- Report actual guest-time, instruction and presentation deltas. Personally
  inspect screenshots for a physical x. Faster serial replies or startup
  dialog disappearance are not physical response.

  Do not repeat a divider sweep or call timer adjustment a fix.
- If physical x appears, the eventual remedy remains separately gated by the
  unchanged 120-second physical nonce/readback and real GUI acceptance.
- Fresh critic independently checks the matched comparison and limitations.
