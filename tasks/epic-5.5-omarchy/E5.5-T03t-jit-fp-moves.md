## Boundary

Extend the existing JIT state handoff for exactly FSGNJ.S/FSGNJN.S/FSGNJX.S,
FMV.W.X and FMV.X.W. Emit integer bit operations only. These instructions are
present in the independently verified current renderer profile; this is the
first narrow hybrid permitted by `docs/jit-fp-policy.md`, not a full FP JIT.
All arithmetic, comparisons, loads/stores and double-precision operations remain
interpreted in this slice. No responsiveness claim follows from opcode support.

The inseparable boundary is F-register state across interpreter, native compiled
modules and browser direct chains: boxed results, raw moves, FS state, exact
mid-block traps and versioned copy elision must agree. Reuse the reserved FPR
handoff range; keep all existing integer/exit/chain offsets stable.

  directed corner cases and varied deterministic seeds agree with the interpreter
