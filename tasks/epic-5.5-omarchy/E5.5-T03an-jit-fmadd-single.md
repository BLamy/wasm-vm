## Boundary

Compile only FMADD.S through a pure packed-result helper using the existing
integer-only F32::fma backend. Reuse precise FS/rounding checks, independent
three-source NaN-box validation and boxed result/flags publication. Allocate
its optional import after the current helpers and retain the actual index.
Other fused operations, double precision, interpreter scheduling and guest
rendering configuration stay unchanged. This is the next narrow measured
hybrid allowed by docs/jit-fp-policy.md, not an Epic6 architecture expansion.

T03am failed all35 nonce reads despite67.2662% compiled retirements and zero
full-map refusals. The verified T03s raw profile contains1278792 opcode0x43
retirements in99998678 instructions, previously omitted from the claim that
measured FP coverage was complete. Its complete64-byte region histogram has
zero drops. The saved renderer page contains24 FMADD.S/dynamic-rm parcels in
those hot regions, while the translator still rejects the entire block on
this family. Rebind the page to the pinned R3 RAM/page walk before relying on
it. The exact AL post-failure profile additionally samples F32::fma at1.5445%
self/2.4223% inclusive. These observations justify testing this boundary;
they do not establish a speedup or imply that FMA helper self time is the
whole benefit of allowing its surrounding blocks to compile.
