entire visible desktop into the browser page. This is rendering recovery only:

- RAM, overlay delta, base manifest and kernel remain exact and paired; corrupt
  or mixed artifacts fail closed, without legacy fallback or personal state.
- The real snapshot repaints before guest execution; recorded `guestRunCalls=0`
  and an inspected screenshot show the actual Omarchy bar and Foot window.
- Fresh built-page launch, same-tab reload and smaller/larger/aspect-changed
  viewports show the whole desktop without clipping or clearing its pixels.
  This is host-side scaling, not a guest modeset claim.
- Absolute pointer coordinates use the displayed canvas rectangle, including
  letterboxing. Deterministic geometry tests and real browser pointer frames
  cover the transform without claiming responsive guest event handling.
- BusyBox/Alpine/Docker retain their IDE layout. The actual browser capability
  suite remains green and no non-favicon console errors are introduced.
- Publish the coherent release to existing Cloudflare R2/Pages, then record and
  inspect actual production initial/reload/resized screenshots, build/artifact
  identities and service-worker identity. No merge is authorized by this task.
