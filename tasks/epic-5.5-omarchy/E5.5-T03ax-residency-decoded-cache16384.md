## Boundary

T03aw exercised the existing `cap-1024` compiled-module residency policy and
reduced eviction/retranslation churn, but the fixed physical trial still ended
with the empty prompt. Its runtime rebuilt 22.9 million decoded blocks while
the decoded cache remained at 4096 entries. The production loader and WASM
already support the independently existing 16384-entry decoded cache. Test
that cache at the same cap-1024 prepared pair as one explicit candidate.

This is a harness-only comparison. Do not change JIT translation, residency or
decoded-cache implementation, admission threshold, recycling counters, guest
settings, resolution, clock divider, input path, product budgets, or deployment
defaults. The ordinary path and T03aw's cap-1024 selection remain unchanged.
