## Boundary

Observation only at the existing physical block-discovery boundary. No
hotness/eviction/translation policy change, new instruction support, renderer
change, production deployment or Epic 6 architecture work. This supplies the
specific missing witness from T03h after the bounded T03i timer trial.

The probe is default off and holds at most 32 distinct refusal identities,
each at most the existing 128-op block bound. It records actual recurring
full-hotness-map refusals with physical entry, generation, exact bytes/op
lengths and bounded counters. Report first-seen selection and overflow;
absence from this bounded sample cannot prove a block never runs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Disabled probe performs no allocation or guest-memory access and does not
  alter discovery/compile decisions, JIT behavior or architectural state.
- Retained identities do not combine changed bytes or generations. Count
  actual refused entries, not sampled 64-byte PC regions. Keep counts-full,
  counting, nomination/dedup/exclusion and FIFO-overflow meanings distinct.
- Report exact-request discovery/compile membership at the observation point;
  never equate nominal Queued state with current queue or installed residency.
  Use the real browser translator's existing eligibility predicate. Label
  report-time residency separately from an exact historical install claim.
- Reuse existing JIT statistics for bounded output. One explicit diagnostic
  boot opt-in may arm the probe; do not add a general-purpose mutation RPC,
  implicit profiling/timer mode, or production default. Expose actual enabled
  state. Keep u64 physical addresses/counters precise in the JS representation.
- Deterministic tests exercise recurring eligible refusals, unsupported and
  excluded contrasts, exact queue identity, capacity overflow, invalidation,
  changed bytes, and disabled/enabled trace/state plus nomination parity.
- Record bounded browser observation on the unchanged R3 LP1 pair, divider64 and
  default JIT/cache policies, binding changed core/probe identities. Produce a
  recurrent eligible refusal witness and bounded contribution data, or an
  explicit missing/inconclusive result. Do not infer GUI responsiveness from
  the probe or claim an admission fault explains the whole stall.
- Fresh Daybreak critic checks the witness and changed observation boundary.

- **J2 — actual refusal and exact identity.** Repeated entries that reach the
  full-count-map return increment the matching retained identity; ordinary
  counting and already-decided dedup entries do not. Same-PC changed bytes,

  Invalidation/reset behavior must preserve that separation, whether old

  with at most 128 walked ops each, without an unbounded auxiliary seen-set.
  Recurrence of retained entries remains countable after capacity fills.
  Overflow counters state their unit; dropped entry observations cannot be
  called a count of distinct omitted identities. First-seen selection is not
  a heavy-hitter census, and absence from it is inconclusive.
- **J4 — exact queues, not nominal history.** Report-time discovery FIFO and
  compile-queue membership compare the captured request identity, not PC or
  nominal Queued state alone. An adversarial stale request at the same PC
  must not match a newer-generation witness. Existing invalidation leaves
  stale FIFO requests (`dispatch.rs:599–611`), and FIFO overflow records

  not a copied approximation or the discovery exclusion flag alone. Current

  PC residency at report time, not proof that this captured byte/generation
  identity was installed, nor proof of any historical install.

  not drain queues, invoke mutating install validation, compile blocks, clear
  witnesses or change discovery counters. Arming is an explicit boot option

  controller mutation RPC or implicit profiling mode. Reports expose actual
  enabled state and lossless u64 addresses/counters across Rust/JS/JSON.
