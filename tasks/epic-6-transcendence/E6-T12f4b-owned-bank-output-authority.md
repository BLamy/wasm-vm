## Boundary

Captured gradients copy bank-derived alpha into ordinary floating outputs.
The existing finite-bank domain permits subnormals and grants numerical access
without ordinary raster output authority. F2/F3 numeric alpha projections are
explicitly authored ports and cannot close the originals.

Establish a bounded use-site certificate and closed owned-bank domain for the
copied output words. Admit only the existing normal-or-signed-zero raster
contract, preserving exact words internally. Check the same immutable bank
generation before effects and on restoration. Keep finite, indirect, count,
radial and PRECISE obligations mandatory. Do not grant output authority to
arbitrary finite words, manufacture values, rewrite alpha, or treat integer
count words as floating output data.

## Deterministic acceptance

`make verify-E6-T12f4b` records domain neighbors, both zero signs,
subnormal/NaN/Inf rejection before effects, consumed bank identity, combined
contracts and replacement/async/restoration. Record original gradient hashes,

## Adversarial verification

erase a combined contract or mistake count data for raster data. Removing the
actual guard must admit a disallowed word and fail an independent domain oracle
before unsafe GPU execution. No NaN/subnormal raster transport claim is allowed.
