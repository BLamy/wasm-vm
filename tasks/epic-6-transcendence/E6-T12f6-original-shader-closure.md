## Boundary

Integrate the separately verified equality, observable-definedness, radial
admission and PRECISE boundaries without adding another runtime feature family.
All19 original SHA-256 bodies must translate unchanged, retain checked metadata,
and compile/link in actual WebGL2. Record every remaining runtime admission
contract explicitly; neither stripped derivatives nor recaptured replacements
count. Exercise the original loop bodies only under their proved count/address
contract and radial bodies only under their explicit definedness domain.

This task is the sole replacement for E6-T12f's full-corpus closure and unlocks
format/view work. Shader closure does not itself enable production negotiation
or establish a complete compositor/performance claim.

## Deterministic acceptance

`make verify-E6-T12f6` binds all 19 original hashes and complete native/Wasm single/
pair results, actual WebGL2 compile/link and independent bit/ULP/pixel observations
for every newly supported original path. Include source/toolchain/output identities,
combined admission, retained shader/scene leaves, exact-source recording and a
final pristine-clone run. Report any real-workload suitability limit separately.

## Adversarial verification

Attack original hash substitution, omitted modifiers, stale or dropped domain
records, unsafe loop counts, undefined radial inputs, contraction and output
interpretation. Alter an exercised semantic mechanism and require independent
failure. Do not equate compile/link counts with full guest offload or general
support for formats/state outside the separately verified boundary.
