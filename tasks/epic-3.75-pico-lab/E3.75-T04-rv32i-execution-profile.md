## Goal
Decode and execute RV32I programs, including control flow, loads/stores, fences, system traps,
and all 32-bit wrap/sign rules, without admitting RV64-only encodings.

## Context
Most encoding fields are shared with RV64, but legal instructions and edge behavior differ:
shift immediates are five bits, XLEN is 32, `LWU`, `LD`, `SD`, and `OP-IMM-32`/`OP-32` are
illegal, and every register result is naturally 32-bit.

## Deliverables
- RV32I decode/execute paths selected by the XLEN profile.
- Directed and architectural tests covering every RV32I instruction and illegal RV64 encoding.
- A bare RV32 web micro-test and live Pico CPU capability entry in `web/`.
- `make verify-E3.75-T04`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T04` passes official RV32I signatures natively and under wasm32.
- [ ] PC, branches, JAL/JALR, effective addresses, signed/unsigned compares, and shift edges match
  a pinned reference trace.
- [ ] Every RV64-only instruction class traps illegal in RV32 without modifying architectural
  state, while the RV64 suite remains unchanged.
- [ ] The built browser page completes its RV64 suite and RV32I micro-test with zero console
  errors and marks RV32I `live`.

## Adversarial verification
Attack PC wrap at `0xffff_fffe`, negative branch extremes, shift amounts 31/32/63, misaligned
targets, faulting stores, and raw RV64-only words. Differentially compare generated streams to the
pinned RV32 oracle. One upper-bit leak or partial side effect on an illegal/faulting instruction
refutes.
