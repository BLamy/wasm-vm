## Goal
Turn “emulate a Raspberry Pi Pico 2” into one versioned, testable machine contract before any
RV32 or RP2350 code is added.

## Context
The target is the non-wireless `pico2` board: RP2350A, 4 MiB external flash, two Hazard3 cores.
The authoritative sources are the official
[RP2350 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2350/rp2350-datasheet.pdf),
[Pico 2 board datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/pico/pico-2-datasheet.pdf),
[Pico SDK](https://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-sdk), and open
[Hazard3 RTL](https://github.com/Wren6991/Hazard3). This epic deliberately stops short of
full-chip coverage; ambiguity about unsupported peripherals would otherwise make every later
acceptance criterion movable.

## Deliverables
- `docs/design/pico2-rp2350-scope.md` with the board identity, reset/boot contract, ISA and memory
  profile, in-scope peripherals, explicit exclusions, and capstone workload.
- `tools/pico/compatibility.toml` with pinned source URLs/revisions and one owner task for every
  required CPU extension, address region, interrupt source class, and peripheral.
- `tools/verify/e3-75-t01-scope.sh` plus `make verify-E3.75-T01`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T01` rejects missing owners, duplicate owners, unpinned references, or a
  capability listed as both supported and excluded.
- [ ] The contract includes RV32I/M/A/C, Zba/Zbb/Zbs/Zbkb, Zcb/Zcmp, Xh3power, Xh3bextm,
  Xh3irq, Xh3pmpm, 520 KiB SRAM, 4 MiB flash, two harts, and all capstone peripherals.
- [ ] USB, secure boot/OTP/access-control enforcement, SWD/debug, Arm mode, Pico 2 W radio, and
  general SPI/I2C/PWM/ADC/HSTX are named follow-ups rather than implied support.

## Adversarial verification
Cross-check the ledger against the RP2350 datasheet chapters for Hazard3, the address map,
bootrom, SIO, interrupts, DMA, and PIO. Find one SDK startup access or capstone behavior with no
owner task, or one exclusion that the capstone secretly requires. Either finding refutes.
