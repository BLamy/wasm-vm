## Goal
Validate a standard Pico 2 UF2 as untrusted input and atomically populate the 4 MiB emulated
flash image without starting a core.

## Context
Browser users will drop arbitrary files onto the emulator. UF2 block metadata, counts, addresses,
family IDs, payload sizes, duplicates, and sparse layouts must be bounded and validated before
any new flash becomes visible. IMAGE_DEF selection and CPU handoff are a separate task.

## Deliverables
- A no-std-friendly UF2 parser for the supported RP2350 family/target form.
- Validation for magic, block numbering/counts, payload lengths, addresses, overlaps,
  equal/conflicting duplicates, total size, and required populated ranges.
- Atomic staging/commit with typed errors shared by CLI and WASM.
- Mutation corpus, browser file-load test, and `make verify-E3.75-T17a`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T17a` loads the pinned UF2 to the expected 4 MiB flash digest in native
  and wasm32 builds.
- [ ] Truncated, huge, sparse-required-range, overlapping, conflicting-duplicate, wrong-family,
  and out-of-flash inputs fail before committed flash changes.
- [ ] Block order and equal duplicates do not change the final digest.
- [ ] Browser rejection is recoverable, leaks no object URL/buffer, and reports UF2 parsing
  `live` with zero console errors.

## Adversarial verification
Fuzz every header field and size arithmetic, shuffle blocks, append trailing data, forge counts,
and fail allocation/commit. Confirm the prior flash image remains byte-identical after every
error. Any partial install, unbounded allocation, integer wrap, or native/WASM error drift refutes.
