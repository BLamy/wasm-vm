## Goal
Enforce the RP2350 Hazard3 protection profile: eight NAPOT PMP regions, a 32-byte granule, and
Xh3pmpm's unlocked M-mode regions.

## Context
The existing RV64 profile supports a larger PMP shape and Linux-oriented rules. RP2350 exposes
eight regions and configures the custom Xh3pmpm extension. Fetch, load, store, and atomic/DMA
access checks must use the correct privilege and priority rules at every byte boundary.

## Deliverables
- Eight-region PMP CSRs with exact reset, WARL, NAPOT encoding, 32-byte granularity, priority,
  lock, and M/U permission behavior.
- Xh3pmpm handling isolated to the Hazard3 profile.
- Boundary/property tests, official/Hazard3 vectors, browser micro-test, and
  `make verify-E3.75-T10b`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T10b` passes pinned PMP vectors in native and wasm32 builds.
- [ ] Fetch/load/store/atomic checks cover first/last/outside bytes, overlapping entries,
  unmatched addresses, lock transitions, and every permission combination.
- [ ] Xh3pmpm changes only the documented M-mode rule and cannot affect RV64 or U mode.
- [ ] Faults are precise and the browser panel marks PMP `live` with zero console errors.

## Adversarial verification
Enumerate addresses around every 32-byte boundary, overlapping priorities, all NAPOT masks, and
lock/write sequences from M and U modes. Attempt accesses crossing a region in one operation.
Any partial write, permissive wrap, wrong first-match rule, or cross-profile leak refutes.
