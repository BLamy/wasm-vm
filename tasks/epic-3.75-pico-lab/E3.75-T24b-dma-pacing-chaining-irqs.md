## Goal
Compose sixteen DMA channels under deterministic priority/arbitration, DREQ pacing, chaining,
abort, and four shared IRQ outputs.

## Context
RP2350 expanded DMA to 16 channels and four IRQs. Inter-channel order is architectural when
channels touch the same memory or peripheral; the emulator needs a recorded tie rule rather than
depending on host iteration or worker order.

## Deliverables
- Sixteen channel instances with documented priority arbitration and fairness.
- Permanent/synthetic/UART DREQ pacing, chain-to, multi-channel trigger/abort, raw/masked/forced
  completion/error interrupts, and reset behavior.
- Concurrent copy, paced UART, chain, cyclic-chain rejection, IRQ, varied-quanta, and browser
  tests.
- `make verify-E3.75-T24b`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T24b` passes frozen multi-channel, pacing, chain, abort, and IRQ traces
  natively and under wasm32.
- [ ] DREQ withdrawal stops before the next beat; reassertion resumes once; chains trigger once;
  cyclic/self chains follow the explicit bounded policy.
- [ ] Equal-priority channels make deterministic progress without starvation and route completion
  to exactly the configured IRQ outputs.
- [ ] The browser panel marks 16-channel DMA `live` with zero console errors.

## Adversarial verification
Run all channels against shared addresses, toggle DREQ at beat boundaries, build chain cycles,
abort multiple channels together, and clear/mask/force IRQs at completion. Vary quantum and
replay. Any starvation, extra trigger, duplicated/lost beat, wrong IRQ, or host-order dependence
refutes.
