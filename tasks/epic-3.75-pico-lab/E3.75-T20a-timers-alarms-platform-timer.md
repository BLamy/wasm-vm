## Goal
Provide one deterministic timebase powering the RP2350 timer/alarm blocks and each Hazard3
hart's RISC-V platform timer interrupt.

## Context
Pico SDK alarms and sleeps combine MMIO timers with core-local timer interrupts. Browser wall
clock and background throttling cannot be architectural inputs; time must advance from recorded
emulator events and support deterministic WFI fast-forward.

## Deliverables
- Timer counters, compare alarms, latch/read semantics, pause/debug controls, raw/masked IRQs,
  and per-hart RISC-V platform timers.
- A shared event scheduler with deterministic instruction-time and explicit recorded host-time
  injection.
- WFI fast-forward, snapshot/restore, wraparound, IRQ fixtures, browser test, and
  `make verify-E3.75-T20a`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T20a` passes frozen counter/alarm/IRQ traces natively and under wasm32.
- [ ] Same-tick alarms, past compares, split-register latch order, wrap, cancel/rearm, and
  per-hart timer outcomes match asserted vectors.
- [ ] WFI skips to the next eligible event without skipping a higher-priority interrupt.
- [ ] Repeated browser runs produce the same alarm UART/pin timeline and mark timers `live`.

## Adversarial verification
Schedule events at now−1/now/now+1 and wrap boundaries, interleave register halves, mask and clear
at delivery, snapshot one tick before expiry, and background the page. Any wall-clock dependence,
lost simultaneous alarm, stale restored event, or incorrect WFI wake refutes.
