## Goal
Provide race-correct hardware spinlocks and global LR/SC invalidation across both harts and
non-hart bus writers.

## Context
SIO spinlocks use read-to-claim/release semantics, while RP2350's global exclusive monitor makes
overlapping peer or DMA writes invalidate reservations. Both are tiny register surfaces with
high-impact concurrency semantics and require chaos evidence.

## Deliverables
- All hardware spinlocks with atomic claim, owner/release/reset behavior, and status.
- A shared exclusive monitor connected to hart loads/stores and a bus-writer invalidation hook
  for DMA.
- Contention/interleave tests, rr chaos recordings, browser micro-test, and
  `make verify-E3.75-T23b`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `make verify-E3.75-T23b` proves every spinlock has at most one owner under all tested
  quanta and chaos recordings.
- [ ] Failed claims are side-effect free; owner/non-owner release and reset follow the frozen
  contract.
- [ ] Peer and synthetic DMA writes invalidate exactly overlapping reservations; unrelated
  accesses do not.
- [ ] Native/WASM traces agree and the browser panel marks locks/exclusives `live`.

## Adversarial verification
Race both harts for every lock, reset an owner, release from the other hart, overlap LR/SC with
peer and DMA-shaped writes at every byte, and vary scheduling quantum. A double owner, false SC
success, stuck lock, cross-address invalidation, or nondeterministic result refutes.
