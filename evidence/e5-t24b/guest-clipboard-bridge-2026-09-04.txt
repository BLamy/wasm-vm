Scope and claim
---------------
The static guest agent now advertises CAP_CLIPBOARD and bridges bounded CLIP_SET/CLIP_GET traffic
through fixed /usr/bin/wl-paste and /usr/bin/wl-copy helpers. Host values are coalesced into one
pending slot, guest changes are emitted as one byte-exact CLIP_SET, and helper failures are
recoverable status with bounded backoff and NAK_CLIPBOARD_UNAVAILABLE. Clipboard state never uses
the serial/UART queue. Invalid UTF-8, 257 KiB input, helper death, missing display, output pressure,
and an in-flight session reset are covered by deterministic fixtures.
