# E5-T26f latency diagnostic — pre-evidence predictions

Recorded after reviewing `e714458a358c0452af0b8eeb21225217803f7f49..2e9d9771477bda265342ac928a25fc6e2d769b25` and before inspecting `evidence/e5-t26f/latency-2e9d9771`.

1. **Diagnostic isolation.** `E5_T26F_DIAGNOSTIC_LATENCY` is accepted only as exact `1` with `E5_T26F_DIAGNOSTIC=reuse`; run metadata remains `acceptance:false`. `make verify-E5-T26f` refuses diagnostic create/reuse before Cargo, Node, web build, or browser work.
2. **Original clock.** `interactionLatency.restoredAt` equals `firstRestore.completedAt`. Installation time is recorded separately and cannot restart either the unchanged 2-second acceptance deadline or the diagnostic's bounded 6-second observation window. Every probe event is reported relative to the original restore clock.
3. **PCM meaning.** `firstWrite` latches the first observed ring-index advance. `firstPcm` latches only a sample with positive written frames, non-silent frames, and amplitude. Because polling is every 50 ms, `firstPcm.observedAt` is an upper-bound observation time, not an exact producer-write timestamp and not proof of completed playback.
4. **Completion remains unchanged.** Successful completion still comes from the existing conditional terminal-marker predicate and accepted guest-visible command record, followed by the existing immediate positive fresh-PCM assertions. The probe must not turn first write, first PCM, AudioContext state, attachment, scheduler progress, or an RPC response into acceptance. The original all-success `postRestoreEnd - postRestoreStart <= 2_000` assertion remains byte-present and operative.
5. **At most one worker RPC.** Only one `schedulerStats()` request may be outstanding; rejected/local `workerRpcStats()` reads, timeout, or repeated timer ticks cannot free that slot or enqueue another worker request. Stop marks a pending sample and late settlement cannot mutate persisted evidence. Since the diagnostic deliberately injects this worker RPC, its timing is localization evidence only and may be probe-perturbed.
6. **Failure-first persistence.** On failure, the original error/phase and current milestones are written before any new page request. Probe stop/collection is bounded to 1 second; collected samples are persisted before state/pixel/serial reads. A stalled collection cannot pile up another page/RPC call, erase the original error, or overwrite evidence after late settlement.
7. **Bounded observation.** PCM/marker samples cap at 120, scheduler samples at 24 and at least 250 ms apart, all timers stop at the original `restoredAt + 6_000`, command settlement, failure, or cleanup. Marker timing records the duration of the existing `state()` predicate without introducing an extra pixel read.

The pending sealed-profile replay can distinguish first PCM observed before marker completion from both being late. It cannot itself verify E5-T26f, waive the 2-second criterion, or replace the existing guest-visible completion and fresh-PCM requirements.
