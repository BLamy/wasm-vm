Model of the claim. With no hotspot from the device, a plane report is
    plane = (guest pointer) - H,   guest pointer = one of the positions the page sent (+-1 rounding)
so the hotspot of an image must be   H = sent_k - plane   for the sent position the guest had
consumed when it placed the plane. The estimator may answer H only when every other (sent - plane)
offset has been excluded; an answer that differs from H by more than one pixel is a wrong answer.
