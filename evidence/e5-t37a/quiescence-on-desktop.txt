E5-T37a round 3 — is the device fabric actually quiescent when the idle Replay desktop waits?

Why it matters: round 3 reports a wait only when the run loop's quiescence marker
(`fabric_idle_epoch == Some(fabric_epoch)`) says every device is idle. If some device on the
real desktop always had work, the guest would never yield and the idle sleep of E5-T37b would
never engage.

How: 2026-11-25, scratch branch `scratch/pacing` (base d106de944) carrying this task's round-2
core and the E5-T37b loader prototype, plus two debug counters in `paced_wait_holds` (calls, and
calls at which the marker said quiescent) exposed through jitStats. Chrome 158 headless, M4 Max,
tools/serve-dev.sh; a Playwright probe sampled three 10 s intervals starting 5 s after the warm
desktop was ready, nobody touching it. The counters and the probe are scratch only; this file
records the probe's console output.

  interval 1 (first 10 s after restore; worker in run slices 69.7% of wall time):
      wait checks 170, of which fabric quiescent 170; guest time x0.50 real; 73 sleeps
  interval 2 (idle; 13.3%):
      wait checks 482, of which fabric quiescent 482; guest time x0.95 real; 196 sleeps
  interval 3 (idle; 11.7%):
      wait checks 398, of which fabric quiescent 398; guest time x0.96 real; 194 sleeps

Reading: 1,050 of 1,050 checks found every device idle. (The counters sat in the round-2
function, which did not yet require quiescence; they show that requiring it would not have
changed one decision in this session.)

Limits: one session, one guest image, no audio playing, no serial input pending, no RTC alarm
armed. With any of those the guest goes round its idle loop instead of sleeping until the
device is idle again — the pre-task behaviour.
