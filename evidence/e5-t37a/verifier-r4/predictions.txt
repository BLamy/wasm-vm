Derivation asked for by the brief (B11), from the Boundary text alone
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Boundary, "Bounded busy lag":
  (a) "host time between two consultations of a guest that did not work in
      between passes in full";
  (b) "the part of it an idle jump cannot hand over at once, because a deadline
      is nearer than the ceiling, stays owed and survives later work";
  (c) "at most max_busy_lag of the time it fell behind may be skipped by idle
      jumps; the rest is forgotten" for a guest that worked.
The worker's scenario as stated in the claim: six consecutive consultations of a
guest that did not work, the host clock 14 ms later at each, a deadline 1 ms
ahead of guest time at each, then a busy stretch with a 50 ms bound.
  - By (a) each consultation's 14 ms pass in full to what the guest may have.
  - The idle jump can hand over only up to the deadline: 1 ms.
  - By (b) the other 13 ms stay owed, and owed time from an earlier consultation
    is still owed (nothing consumed it: each jump used 1 ms of the new 14).
  => owed after consultation k = 13 * k ms: 13, 26, 39, 52, 65, 78.
  - Then the guest works while the host runs far ahead. By (c) it may skip at
    most 50 ms of the time it fell behind while working; by (b) the 78 ms owed
    survive that work. => the next unconstrained idle jump moves guest time by
    78 + 50 = 128 ms, exactly (if the host ran at least 50 ms ahead during the
    work and no deadline is nearer).
  These are the numbers the claim states. They follow from the contract; I do
  not have to take them from the code. What I must still check in the test:
  that the busy stretch really is "work" (> 2048 retired), that the host ran
  more than 50 ms ahead in it (else "one bound" is not what is measured), and
  that the 78 ms are measured as a separate term (a test that only asserts the
  sum 128 could be satisfied by 28 + 100).
  For the mutant (owed = host time since the ANCHOR, not since the previous
  consultation) I expect over-counting from the second consultation on, because
  the anchor does not move by 14 ms per consultation when only 1 ms is handed
  over. So the mutant must already fail at k = 2 (26 ms expected).
